## 2. What Higgsfield can actually make for a channel

Checked directly against the connected Higgsfield tools, not marketing pages.

### Faceless Studio (the "faceless-video" workflow)

| Channel type | Default look | Pacing / tone | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Explainer** | Editorial Motion Graphics or Stickman Cartoon | fast cuts, casual second-person, hook + promise | tech, money, self-improvement |
| **History** | Editorial Motion Graphics; alternates Paper Diorama, Mannequin; long-form Documentary 10+ min in Watercolor Chronicle | chronological, witty storyteller | history, biographies, "how X happened" |
| **Fairy Tale & Myth** | Cinematic Storybook (hand-painted 2D, animated on twos, music bed) | slow, atmospheric, hushed narrator | mythology, folklore, legends |
| **Kids** | Studio 3D or Fluffy Toy; optional sung Song Mode | 4 cuts per 10 s block, warm teacher voice | nursery, learning, songs |
| **Picture Story** | still images with narration | slideshow with voice | any type told in still pictures |

Fixed facts about the output:

- Duration options are 1, 2 or 3 minutes by default (Fairy Tale & Myth 2 or 3). Long-form 10+ minute documentaries exist only for History.
- Aspect ratio is 16:9 or 9:16 only. That is enough for long-form plus Shorts.
- One locked narrator voice per video (ElevenLabs through the platform), burned subtitles, optional thumbnail, optional music bed.
- 21 visual presets available (Editorial Motion Graphics, Stickman Cartoon, Watercolor Chronicle, Fairy Tale & Myth, Paper Diorama, Pastel Flat 2D, Colorful 3D, Hand Drawn, Poster Vector, Mannequin, Whiteboard Doodle, 3D Papercraft, Mixed Media, Low Poly, 2D Illustrator, Pixel Art, Claymotion, Isometric Flat Vector, 3D Mix, Studio 3D, Fluffy Toy, Paper collage).
- Characters never lip-sync; the narrator is always off-screen. That is a feature for a faceless channel.

### YouTube policy you must design around

- Since July 2025 the "inauthentic content" rule demonetises mass-produced, templated videos: static image + AI voice, recycled clips, slideshows with no narrative, scripts read from other sources.
- In January 2026 YouTube terminated a wave of AI channels (16 channels, 4.7B views) and in July 2026 clarified that fabricated upsetting content (fake rescues, fake tragedies) is a target.
- What stays monetised: an original script with a point of view, consistent original visuals, disclosed synthetic voice, and real variation between videos. AI narration over an original script is explicitly allowed.

## 4. Channel concepts you can build with Higgsfield, ranked

### Option A (recommended first): Myth and history storybook channel

- **Higgsfield types:** Fairy Tale & Myth (Cinematic Storybook) for legends, History type for real events. Both 2-3 minute episodes, plus 10+ minute Watercolor Chronicle documentaries once the channel has an audience.
- **Why:** this is the single best-looking thing Higgsfield produces, it is story-driven (where AI video shines and explainers do not), and mythology is one of India's fastest-growing faceless niches while world history pays $6-18 RPM globally.
- **Content lanes:** Indian epics retold one episode at a time (Mahabharata side stories, Panchatantra, regional folklore), then Greek, Norse, Egyptian and Japanese myths. Each episode is one self-contained story with a twist.
- **Second channel for free:** dub every episode to Hindi with the dubbing tool and publish on a Hindi sister channel.
- **Benchmarks:** AI Bible Stories style channels; Indian mythology channels; The Armchair Historian for the documentary tier.
- **Name ideas:** "Tales Before Time", "Once, Long Ago", "Ink & Ember", "Myth, Retold", "The Old Fires", "Forgotten Thrones". Hindi sister channel: "Puraani Kathayein", "Katha Ghar".
- **Title formula:** "The [thing] that [surprising outcome]" or "Why [god/king] [did the unexpected]". Example: "The curse that built the Taj Mahal", "Why Yudhishthira lied only once".

### Option B (recommended second): AI and money explainer channel

- **Higgsfield types:** Explainer in Editorial Motion Graphics (preferred) or Stickman Cartoon. 2-3 minute episodes, then Clipify into Shorts.
- **Why:** AI tutorials are the fastest-growing niche of 2026 and tech CPM is $8-20. You can write these with real authority, which is the only thing that makes a contrarian title believable. This is the plan from `faceless-youtube-strategy.md`.
- **Risk:** stick-figure explainers are crowded and AI-generated ones can look generic. Win on the script and the hook, not the animation.
- **Names:** "simpler than that" (top pick), "the short version", "not that hard", "plainly".
- **First six titles:** AI agents are simpler than that; prompting is simpler than that; learning to code in 2026 is simpler than that; getting a tech job without a degree is simpler than that; your data privacy is simpler than that; building an app with AI is simpler than that.

### Option C: Hindi personal finance for first-time investors

- **Higgsfield type:** Explainer, Pastel Flat 2D or Isometric Flat Vector, Hindi narrator voice.
- **Why:** highest RPM in India (₹150-250), 150M+ demat accounts, Money4India grew to 100K in six months on this exact lane.
- **Risk:** needs careful accuracy and a disclaimer on every video. Keep to education, never tips.
- **Names:** "Paisa, Seedha" , "Sach Mein Simple", "Rupee Clear".

### Option D: Kids learning songs

- **Higgsfield type:** Kids, Studio 3D, Song Mode (real sung song, 1-2 minutes).
- **Why:** kids content scales more than anything else, and Higgsfield has a purpose-built mode.
- **Risk:** made-for-kids videos earn far less per view, no comments, and a brutally competitive field. Only do this with a recurring character and a 50-episode commitment.

### Option E (not recommended): AI animal rescue or emotional fabricated stories

Highest view-to-subscriber ratio of any AI format (3.2M views on a 1.3K-sub channel), but these are the exact videos YouTube's July 2026 clarification targets as fabricated upsetting content. Expect demonetisation or termination. Skip it.

### Option F (watch, do not start yet): vertical AI ShortDrama

Fastest-growing AI format of 2026, mostly new channels. It needs consistent characters across dozens of episodes and lip-sync, which the Faceless Studio does not do. Revisit once Higgsfield's character tools fit, or if Option A's characters prove popular.

## 5. Recommended setup: two channels from one pipeline

1. **Main channel: Option A** in English. One 2-3 minute story every 3-4 days, plus 2-3 Shorts cut from it with Clipify.
2. **Sister channel: Hindi dub** of the same episodes, published a day later.
3. **After 20 episodes:** add one 10-minute History documentary a month (Watercolor Chronicle) as the long-watch-time anchor.
4. **Month 4+:** if the AI explainer idea still appeals, launch Option B as a separate channel. Never mix the two on one channel; the algorithm punishes mixed audiences.

## 6. Production pipeline per episode (about 2 hours of your time)

1. **Pick the story** (15 min): one myth or event with a clear twist. Keep a list of 50 up front.
2. **Script** (45 min): 300-450 words for 2-3 minutes. Cold open on the twist, then the story, then one line of meaning. Cite the source in the description (the History and Explainer validators require sources anyway).
3. **Generate** (hands-off, 20-40 min of waiting): run the Faceless Studio flow with the locked style, locked voice, 16:9, subtitles on, thumbnail on.
4. **QC** (15 min): watch it once. Regenerate any block with a broken scene. Never ship a block you have not watched.
5. **Package** (15 min): title in the formula, 4K thumbnail from the thumbnail workflow using the same framework every time, description with source and AI disclosure.
6. **Shorts** (10 min): Clipify into 3 clips, 9:16, consistent subtitle font and colour.
7. **Dub** (hands-off): Hindi version for the sister channel.

Lock these once and never change them: the style preset, the voice, the subtitle font and colour, the thumbnail framework, the title formula.

Weekly loop: look at retention graphs for the two best and two worst episodes, find the second where viewers leave, and change the script structure, not the visuals, for the next batch.

## 8. Guardrails

- Disclose AI in the upload settings on every video. It does not reduce reach or revenue.
- Every script is original and has a point of view. Never read a Wikipedia article aloud.
- Vary the structure between episodes (cold open, question, in-media-res) so no two look templated.
- One channel = one audience. Myths and AI explainers never share a channel.
- Keep a sources line in every description. For finance and health, add a disclaimer line.
- Do not buy views, subs or use "automation" services that upload for you. The January 2026 wave hit exactly those channels.
