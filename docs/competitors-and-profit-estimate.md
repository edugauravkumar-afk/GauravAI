# Competitors, Their Earnings, and Our Profit Estimate

Research date: 4 October 2026. Stats sites (vidIQ, Social Blade, HypeAuditor, Playboard)
were blocked from this environment, so figures come from their search snippets. Earnings
numbers from these trackers are estimates and often disagree with each other by 5x.
Use them for relative scale, not as facts.

### The three lessons from the competitor set

1. **Reach is easy, ad money is thin.** A 2.4M-sub English myth channel is estimated at ~$5K a month. A 7.8M-sub Hindi myth channel is estimated at $1K to $6K. Mythology viewers are loyal but advertisers pay $5 to $9 per 1,000 US views for folklore, and ₹30 to ₹45 per 1,000 Indian views.
2. **Video length decides RPM more than niche does.** Under 8 minutes a video only gets pre-roll, roughly $1 to $3 RPM. Over 8 minutes mid-rolls lift RPM by 40% to 100%. At 12 to 15 minutes with several mid-rolls, RPM can be 2 to 3 times the short-video figure. The history channels earn 5 to 10 times the myth channels mainly because their videos are long.
3. **Nobody owns "AI-animated myth retellings with a twist" yet.** The big channels are 8 to 15 years old with older animation. The AI ones are tiny. That is the gap.

## 2. Strategy change this research forces

Keep the 2 to 3 minute episodes for discovery, because they are cheap and get recommended. But add an **8 to 12 minute "collection" episode** every week from month 2, built from three or four already-made tales plus new linking narration ("Five times Death lost an argument", "Three kings who were punished for their own wishes"). Collections cost almost no new credits, cross the 8-minute line for mid-rolls, and add the watch hours needed for monetisation.

Revenue per 1,000 views roughly doubles the day this starts.

## 4. Revenue assumptions

Blended RPM (what you actually receive per 1,000 views) for an English mythology channel with a 30% US/UK/CA/AU, 40% India, 30% rest-of-world audience:

| Format | RPM assumed | Why |
|---|---|---|
| English 2 to 3 min episode | $1.50 | pre-roll only, mixed geography |
| English 8 to 12 min collection | $4.00 | mid-rolls, same audience |
| Hindi episode (Katha Ghar) | $0.45 (about ₹38) | Indian mythology RPM ₹30 to ₹45 |
| Shorts, any language | $0.06 | Shorts revenue share is tiny everywhere |

Monetisation does not start before month 4 at the earliest (1,000 subs and 4,000 watch hours, or 10M Shorts views in 90 days). Months 1 to 3 earn nothing.

## 5. Three scenarios, monthly views at month 12

| Scenario | English episodes | English collections | Hindi | Shorts | Monthly ad revenue | Annual cost | Year-1 profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conservative** (channel finds a small audience) | 100K | 50K | 150K | 500K | ~$450 (₹38K) | ~$700 | roughly break-even to ~$1,000 for the year |
| **Base** (what the plan is built to hit) | 400K | 300K | 1M | 3M | ~$2,400 (₹2.0L) | ~$700 | ~$8,000 to $10,000 (₹7L to ₹8.5L) |
| **Strong** (one or two episodes break out) | 1.5M | 1M | 5M | 10M | ~$9,100 (₹7.7L) | ~$1,300 (Ultra) | ~$30,000 to $35,000 (₹25L to ₹30L) |

How the monthly numbers are built, base case:
400K x $1.50 = $600, plus 300K x $4.00 = $1,200, plus 1M x $0.45 = $450, plus 3M x $0.06 = $180. Total about $2,430 a month at month 12. Year-1 profit assumes revenue ramps from zero in month 4 to that figure by month 12, minus about $700 of costs.

What the base case needs: 25 episodes by day 90, 60 to 70 episodes plus 30 collections by month 12, one Short over 1M views, and a Hindi channel that rides the English one.

## 6. Income the trackers do not count

Every competitor above earns more than the AdSense estimate. Add these from month 6:

| Source | Realistic year-1 add | How |
|---|---|---|
| Affiliate book links | $50 to $300 a month | Amazon India links to the Mahabharata, Ramayana, Panchatantra editions in every description |
| Sponsorships | $200 to $2,000 per integration after 50K subs | Audiobook apps, learning apps, Indian ed-tech. OverSimplified-level channels get $10K to $50K each. |
| Channel memberships | small | early access to the next tale |
| Licensing | later | Kids and ed-tech platforms license mythology animation libraries |
| Second channel | multiplier | the Hindi dub doubles inventory at near-zero cost |

A base-case year 2, with 150 episodes, 50 collections, two sponsors a month and affiliates, is a $5K to $8K a month business. That is the realistic target, not year 1.

## 7. Honest risk register for the estimate

- **Ad revenue per video is falling platform-wide.** One 2026 analysis puts average long-form ad revenue per video down about 55% year on year. The base case already uses low RPMs, but the trend is down.
- **Tracker numbers are noisy.** The Divine Tales ranges from $900 to $6,200 a month across three trackers. Our scenarios could be off by 2x in either direction.
- **Months 1 to 3 are pure cost.** About $150 to $200 and 80 hours of your time before the first rupee.
- **Credits per episode are unmeasured.** Episode 1 settles it.
- **The whole upside is in volume and length.** Ten short episodes a month earn pocket money. Ten short episodes plus four collections plus the Hindi mirror is a business.
