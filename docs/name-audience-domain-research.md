## Audience evidence

- 74% (Homebase 2026) to 89% (US Chamber 2026) of small businesses use or pilot AI; only ~17.7% pay for AI.
- Top tasks to automate among AI-using small businesses: marketing content 53%, sales outreach 49%, customer service 46%, admin and scheduling 41%.
- No-code and automation niche on YouTube: about +220% growth in 2026.
- n8n: 230K+ active users, 6x year-on-year growth, $2.5B valuation, r/n8n 200K+ members.

Gap: all are face-on and mainly teach people to sell automation. None serves the business
owner who just wants the task gone, faceless.

## Pricing evidence

- Simple n8n project $300-1,200; multi-workflow $1,500-5,000; retainers $500-1,500/month.
- Upwork n8n postings: median $23/hr, top 10% $45+/hr. India freelancers ₹300-2,000/hr.
