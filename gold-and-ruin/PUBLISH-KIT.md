# Gold and Ruin: publish kit

Everything to paste into YouTube Studio for each documentary. Thumbnails are in
`channel-assets/thumbnails/`. The text thumbnails are the fallback; once a documentary is
generated, replace the coin on the right with the best painted frame from the video and
keep the same text layout.

Rules that never change:
- Title under 60 characters, the surprising fact first, no clickbait that the video does not pay off.
- Description starts with the hook sentence, then chapters, then sources, then the AI disclosure.
- Tick "Altered or synthetic content" at upload. The animation and narration are AI-made.
- Pinned comment asks one question that history people love to argue about.
- Add a Hindi audio track after publishing (YouTube Studio → Subtitles → Audio → Add).

```
In 1324 a king walked out of West Africa with so much gold that when he reached Cairo and started giving it away, the price of gold fell and stayed down for years. This is the story of where that gold came from, what it bought, and why almost none of it was left a few generations later.
```
